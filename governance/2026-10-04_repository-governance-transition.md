# Groundline-Public: Governance Transition

**Date:** 4 October 2026  
**Governance Status:** Active  
**Classification:** Public Governance Record  
**Repository:** Groundline-Public  
**Framework:** DGCP™

## 1. Purpose

This record documents a forward governance transition for `Groundline-Public`.

The transition concerns repository administration and stewardship. It does not change the functional identity of Groundline Research Lab, the publication status of existing records, or the evidentiary meaning of material already published.

## 2. Effective Governance Date

Effective **4 October 2026**, `Groundline-Public` is designated to move from system-level administrative custody to dedicated Groundline Research Lab repository administration.

The administrative location of a repository does not redefine its functional identity.

`Groundline-Public` remains the public publication layer of Groundline Research Lab.

## 3. Historical Integrity

Existing repository history must be preserved.

The transition does not authorize:

- Retrospective rewriting of historical commits.
- Re-dating of earlier publications.
- Replacement of existing publication records solely because repository administration changes.
- Removal of provenance, source history, correction history, or governance history.

Earlier records remain attributable to the governance and repository state under which they were created.

## 4. Publication Continuity

Existing Groundline-Public publication standards remain in force unless a later authorized governance record explicitly changes them.

This includes requirements concerning:

- Evidence.
- Provenance.
- Research state.
- Public and private separation.
- Corrections.
- Historical integrity.
- Rights and licensing.
- Publication boundaries.

A repository transfer does not create a new publication identity for existing records.

## 5. System Authority

System-level architecture and governance remain under:

**P’Toh**  
**System Architect DGCP™**

System-level authority and repository administration are separate functions.

Moving repository administration does not alter the wider DGCP™ governance framework or the role of the System Architect.

## 6. Transition Boundary

This governance record applies only to `Groundline-Public`.

It does not, by itself, transfer or modify other Groundline Research Lab repositories, research programs, operational records, or private working environments.

Any later transition affecting another repository or research function should be documented separately through its own traceable governance record.

## 7. Continuity Principle

The transition follows a forward-only governance principle:

**Existing History → Governance Transition → Continued Groundline Publication**

Repository location may change.

Research identity, publication provenance, and historical continuity remain preserved.
